License

Icon Monks is open source under the MIT license. You can use every icon for free in personal, client and commercial work.

You can

Use the icons in websites, apps, products, templates, presentations, print and merchandise. Modify them, recolor them and redistribute them, including in paid products.

You don't need to

Pay, sign up, or credit Icon Monks in your interface. A mention is always appreciated but never required.

Please

Keep the license file when you redistribute the icon files or packages themselves, for example in an open source project or another icon set.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Icon Monks in commercial projects?

Yes. The MIT license allows use in commercial products, client work, paid apps and templates at no cost.

Do I have to credit Icon Monks?

No credit is needed in your product or interface. If you redistribute the icon files or packages themselves, keep the license notice with them.

Can I modify the icons?

Yes. You can change their shapes, colors and sizes, and combine them with your own artwork.

Can I use the icons in a logo or trademark?

You may, but other people can use the same icons too, so an icon on its own is not a distinctive mark. We recommend customizing it.

Can I include the icons in an icon pack or UI kit I sell?

Yes, as long as the license notice is included with the redistributed icons.
