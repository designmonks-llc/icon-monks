Image Scan icon

A free image scan icon from the Media set, useful for image recognition, scan, photo, detect. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.

SwiftUI
