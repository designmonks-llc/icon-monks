Messenger Logo icon

A free messenger logo icon from the Brands set, useful for messenger, facebook, meta, chat. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
