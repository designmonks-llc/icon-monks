Hard Drive icon

A free hard drive icon from the Devices set, useful for storage, disk, hdd, ssd. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.

React

Vue

</script>

Icon font

SwiftUI
