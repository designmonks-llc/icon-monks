Video Camera Pencil icon

A free video camera pencil icon from the Media set, useful for video editor, edit video, media, clip. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
