Floppy Disk Slash icon

A free floppy disk slash icon from the Files set, useful for save off, no save, floppy disk, disabled. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.

<FloppyDiskSlash size={24} />

<script setup>

</script>
