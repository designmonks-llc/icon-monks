Exclamation Mark icon

A free exclamation mark icon from the Symbols set, useful for exclamation, alert, important, bang. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
