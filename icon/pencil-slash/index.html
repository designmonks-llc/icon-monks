Pencil Slash icon

A free pencil slash icon from the Editor set, useful for read only, no editing, locked edit, edit off. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
