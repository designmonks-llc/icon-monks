Filing Cabinet icon

A free filing cabinet icon from the Files set, useful for cabinet, drawers, archive, storage. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
