Film Strip Slash icon

A free film strip slash icon from the Media set, useful for no video, movie off, film off, disabled. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
