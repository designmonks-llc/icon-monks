Creative Commons By icon

A free creative commons by icon from the Symbols set, useful for attribution, cc by, license, credit. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
