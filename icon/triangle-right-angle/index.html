Triangle Right Angle icon

The triangle right angle icon is a free design icon from Icon Monks, drawn as one clean path on a 256-unit grid in six weights. It is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.

About the triangle right angle icon

It is a right angle variant of the triangle icon, so it keeps the exact outline of the triangle icon. Use it to stand for right triangle, triangle and right angle. The regular weight is a single path made of 3 shapes, and the duotone weight adds a 20% tint behind the outline.

Pairs well with the cone, cylinder and diamond minus icons, which share the “geometry” theme. Use the same weight for all of them so they read as one set.

When to use the triangle right angle icon

It belongs to the design set, made for design tools, editors and creative apps. People find it by searching for “right triangle”, “triangle”, “right angle”, “geometry”, “math” and “shape”. Pair it with the related icons below so a screen stays in one visual style.

Questions about the triangle right angle icon

Is the triangle right angle icon free for commercial use?

Yes. The triangle right angle icon is released under the MIT license, so you can use it in personal, client and commercial projects without paying or adding credit in your interface.

How do I change the color and size of the triangle right angle icon?

The icon draws in currentColor, so it takes the text color around it. In React, Vue and the web component, set the size and color props; in the icon browser, pick a color and size before you download a PNG, WebP, SVG or PDF.

What is the difference between the triangle right angle and triangle icons?

The triangle right angle icon is a right angle variant of the triangle icon. Both share the same outline and come in all six weights, so you can swap one for the other to show a change of state.
