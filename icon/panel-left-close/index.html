Panel Left Close icon

A free panel left close icon from the Interface set, useful for collapse sidebar, hide panel, left panel. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
