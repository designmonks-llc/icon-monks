Chart Candlestick icon

A free chart candlestick icon from the Charts set, useful for stock, candlestick, trading, market. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.

Vue

</script>

Web component

Icon font

SwiftUI
