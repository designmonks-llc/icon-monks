Person Pilgrim icon

A free person pilgrim icon from the People set, useful for hajj, haji, pilgrim, ihram. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
