Briefcase Warning icon

A free briefcase warning icon from the Business set, useful for job alert, urgent, work warning, briefcase. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
