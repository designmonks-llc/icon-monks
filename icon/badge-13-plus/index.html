Badge 13 Plus icon

The badge 13 plus icon is a free letters & numbers icon from Icon Monks, drawn as one clean path on a 256-unit grid in six weights. It is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.

About the badge 13 plus icon

It is a 13 plus variant of the badge icon, so it keeps the exact outline of the badge icon. Use it to stand for age rating, 13+ and adult. The regular weight is a single path made of 5 shapes, and the duotone weight adds a 20% tint behind the outline.

Pairs well with the alpha, alphabet arabic and star circle icons. Use the same weight for all of them so they read as one set.

When to use the badge 13 plus icon

It belongs to the letters & numbers set, made for labels, badges, counters and alphabets. People find it by searching for “age rating”, “13+”, “adult” and “restriction”. Pair it with the related icons below so a screen stays in one visual style.

Questions about the badge 13 plus icon

Is the badge 13 plus icon free for commercial use?

Yes. The badge 13 plus icon is released under the MIT license, so you can use it in personal, client and commercial projects without paying or adding credit in your interface.

How do I change the color and size of the badge 13 plus icon?

The icon draws in currentColor, so it takes the text color around it. In React, Vue and the web component, set the size and color props; in the icon browser, pick a color and size before you download a PNG, WebP, SVG or PDF.

What is the difference between the badge 13 plus and badge icons?

The badge 13 plus icon is a 13 plus variant of the badge icon. Both share the same outline and come in all six weights, so you can swap one for the other to show a change of state.
