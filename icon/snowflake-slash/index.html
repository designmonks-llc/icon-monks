Snowflake Slash icon

A free snowflake slash icon from the Weather set, useful for cooling off, ac off, no snow, cold off. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.

</script>
