Skip Back icon

A free skip back icon from the Media set, useful for previous, track, back, music. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.

</script>

SwiftUI

Compose
