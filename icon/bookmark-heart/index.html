Bookmark Heart icon

A free bookmark heart icon from the Interface set, useful for bookmark, favorite, heart, like. It comes in six weights and is free for personal and commercial use under the MIT license.

Open in the icon browserCustomize size and color, then download PNG, WebP or PDF.
